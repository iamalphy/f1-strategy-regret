F1 Strategy Regret Index

Which team threw away the most points on strategy in 2024?

McLaren lost the most points to strategy (48 points) across 20 modelled races.

Each driver's race is replayed under alternative 1- and 2-stop strategies, holding the rest of the field fixed. Tyre degradation and pit loss are estimated from the race data itself, separately for green-flag and safety-car stops. The best available strategy sets the benchmark; regret is the points difference between that benchmark and what the driver actually scored. The replay reproduces the real finishing order with a mean Spearman rank correlation of 0.88. It is a hindsight benchmark, not a prediction - see the README for limitations.
